1. What North Way is

North Way is a free, independent travel guide for René Mouawad (Qlayaat) Airport and the north of Lebanon. It is not run by the airport, an airline, or the government. Information is for information only and can be out of date. Always check with your airline, hotel or driver before you travel.

2. Using the app

  • The app is free to download and use. We do not charge guests any fee. You pay only the price shown by the business.
  • Hotels, restaurants, taxis and car rental companies are independent businesses. North Way does not own or run them and is not responsible for the service they give. The business confirms your booking and provides the service.
  • Prices are set by each business and may change. A booking request is not a guarantee until the business confirms it.
  • Do not misuse the app: no false bookings, no fake ratings, no spam, no attempts to break or overload it.

3. Bookings and payment

  • For restaurants you book a table for free and pay the restaurant directly.
  • Hotels, taxis and car rentals: by ticking the agreement box before you book, you agree that you pay the provider through North Way (by Whish) and that North Way holds your payment until you arrive at the hotel, take the car, or get in the taxi. The provider confirms this by scanning your QR code. After that, North Way passes the payment to the provider. North Way keeps its commission from the provider, never from you. If the provider does not confirm, ask for a refund as explained below.
  • I agree that the payment for any provider I book from the app is held by North Way until the provider confirms my booking by scanning my QR code.
  • Restaurants: there is no payment through North Way. You book a table for free and pay the restaurant directly. Restaurants pay North Way a yearly fee after their free year.
  • You confirm this agreement by ticking the box in the Hotels, Taxis and Car rental tabs and in the booking form. The Book buttons do not work until you tick it.
  • To cancel or ask for a refund, write to northwaylb@gmail.com with your booking code. Refunds follow the cancellation rule of the business.

4. What data we collect

  • Booking: your name, phone number, date, number of guests or rooms, and your note. For a taxi from "my current location", the pickup point is also sent to the taxi driver so he can find you.
  • Ratings: only a guest whose booking the business confirmed by scanning the QR code can rate. We store your stars (and an optional short comment that is never published) with the booking code, one rating per booking. Your phone number is not stored in the ratings list.
  • Problem reports: the reason, your note and the contact you choose to give.
  • Provider sign-up: the business name, phone, email, Google Maps link, price and note that the business sends.
  • On your device only: your language, saved places, your booking list, and your location for "near me" and "how far" (your location is not sent to us for these, except for a taxi price or booking as explained above).
  • We use no advertising, no tracking and no analytics cookies.

5. How we use and keep it

  • Booking details are sent to the business you chose so it can contact you, and are kept in North Way's private booking sheet (Google Sheets) to run the service and keep payment records.
  • Guest name, phone and note are automatically erased 180 days after the booking. Payment records (amounts, dates, booking code, without your phone number) are kept for our accounts as the law requires.
  • We never sell your data. We share it only with the business you booked, with our service provider Google (which hosts the sheet and sends emails), and with the authorities if the law requires.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to northwaylb@gmail.com with your booking code. We answer within 30 days. We also count visits and installs of the app as anonymous daily totals (language and phone type only). No name, phone, email or ID is saved for this count.

7. Providers (businesses)

  • You must give true details: name, phone, Google Maps place, and prices.
  • The first 20 hotels, taxis and car rentals joining North Way get a free subscription for one year, with no commission. After the free year they pay a commission on bookings made through the app.
  • Restaurants that join on or before 31 December 2027 are free for one year from their joining date. After that they pay $60 per year. Restaurants that join later pay from the start. If a restaurant does not pay, it is removed from the app.
  • We may hide or remove a business that gives false information, receives serious complaints, or does not respect a booking.
  • Fake or false information: a business that gives fake or false information (a fake identity, a fake place, a fake phone or Whish number, or fake documents) is removed from the app. We do not pay it any money held for bookings, and we may refund the guests. North Way may also report the case to the authorities. North Way may ask for proof of the business (such as a photo, a license, or an ID) before it lists it.

8. Limits

We work to keep the app correct and available, but we cannot promise it will always be error-free or online. To the extent the law allows, North Way is not liable for losses that come from the service given by a business or from information that was out of date.

9. Points and rewards

Guests: you get 1 point for each booking that the business confirms by scanning your QR code. With 30 points you get 30% off North Way's own commission on your next booking paid through the app (the business's share does not change). Each point is valid for 1 year from the day it is given and then ends. Points have no cash value, cannot be sold or transferred, and come back if a booking is cancelled. We may limit or cancel points that come from false bookings or abuse. Your points belong to your verified email; you can see them in the app with your booking code and phone number, on any device. Points need a verified email: we send a 6-digit code to your email, and we keep only a scrambled (hashed) copy of the email, not the email itself.

Providers: a provider gets 1 point for each new provider he brings who is checked and accepted by North Way (not himself, not a provider already listed). Every 10 points give 1 month free (no monthly fee or no commission, or one more free month in the free year). We may cancel points from false or duplicate sign-ups.

مزوّدو الخدمة: يحصل المزوّد على نقطة عن كل مزوّد جديد يحضره وتتحقق منه طريق الشمال وتقبله (ليس نفسه ولا مزوّداً مسجلاً من قبل). كل 10 نقاط تعطي شهراً مجانياً (بدون رسوم شهرية أو بدون عمولة، أو شهر إضافي في السنة المجانية). يجوز لنا إلغاء النقاط الناتجة عن تسجيلات وهمية أو مكررة.
